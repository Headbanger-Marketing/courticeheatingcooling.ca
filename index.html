Equipment Care

How to Plan Heating and Cooling Maintenance

Start with the installed equipment and its manufacturer instructions. Maintenance needs depend on the appliance and its use. Keep service records and ask a qualified contractor which seasonal inspections apply.

ENERGY STAR maintenance checklist

  1. Inspect user-serviceable filters and clean or replace them as the equipment instructions specify.
  2. For heating, discuss the controls, airflow or heat delivery, and any fuel-burning appliance inspection.
  3. For cooling, discuss coils, airflow, refrigerant assessment where applicable, and condensate drainage.
  4. Leave internal electrical, refrigerant, fuel, and combustion work to a qualified contractor.

Preparing a Request in Courtice

How do I plan fireplace installation in Courtice for a fireplace fault noticed after other trades finished work?

When requesting work for a property in Courtice, weigh repairing the existing fireplace, adjusting how it was fitted, and installing a different unit against what the contractor confirms. The contractor's findings should keep what concerns the fireplace itself apart from what concerns the surrounding work before a choice is made.

Which fireplace symptom began after the other trade finished work nearby?

Note the first symptom, its date, and any nearby flooring, painting, cabinet, or wiring work. Do not open the unit. If gas odour or smoke appears, stop using it, leave, and seek qualified help.

Which details about the other trade's work should be recorded before a fireplace request?

Write down each trade that worked near the fireplace, the room, and the dates the work started and finished. Include framing, finishing, or wiring work around the hearth, mantel, or wall.

How should the first sign of a fireplace problem be described alongside that work?

Record the day the problem was first noticed, such as a weak flame, a draught, soot, or a unit that no longer responds. Report the timing as an observation, not as a conclusion about cause.

Before a fireplace is installed or replaced after other work, what should the contractor confirm?

Ask the contractor to name what was tested and what finding supports each recommendation. A new installation or replacement should follow that finding, not the timing of the other work alone.
